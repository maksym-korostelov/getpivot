The service

Pivot helps you plan and track personal goals, with optional AI-assisted suggestions.

Plans and AI allowance

Each plan comes with an allowance for AI features. The current allowance for each plan is shown in the app. When your allowance runs out, AI features pause until it refills or you change plan; your goals and tasks stay available.

  • Free: your allowance refills at the start of each calendar month.
  • Guest: a guest account has a one-time allowance that does not refill. To subscribe, sign in with an account first.
  • Pro: a monthly subscription that renews automatically until you cancel it. Your allowance refills to the full Pro amount on each renewal date, not on the calendar month.

Changing plans never changes your existing goals, milestones or tasks. They stay exactly as they are.

Subscriptions

You buy a subscription from Apple through the App Store. Apple handles payment, renewal, cancellation and refunds under its own terms. The price and billing period are shown in the app and by Apple before you buy. We never receive your payment details.

  • Cancelling: you can turn off auto-renewal at any time in your Apple subscription settings, which the app links to. You keep Pro until the end of the period you have paid for, and your account then moves to Free.
  • If a renewal payment doesn't go through: you keep Pro while Apple retries the payment, and the app will suggest checking your payment method. If the payment can't be completed, your account moves to Free.
  • Refunds: if Apple refunds a subscription, Pro ends straight away and your account moves to Free. Any allowance you have already used is never charged back to you.
