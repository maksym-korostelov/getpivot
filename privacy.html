Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2026

This policy explains what Pivot collects, why, and who else sees it. It covers the Pivot iOS app and this website.

Who we are

The controller of your personal data is Maksym Korostelov, Polanka 51, 32-400 Myślenice, Poland. You can reach us at maksym.korostelov.dev@gmail.com.

What we collect

Account information

You can use Pivot as a guest, with no sign-up. A guest account stores an anonymous identifier and usage records (how much of your monthly AI allowance you've used) with our backend provider, Supabase. We do not store an email address or a password for a guest account.

Because a guest account has no email or password, it can't be recovered if you lose or replace your device — registering is the way to keep your account recoverable.

If you register with an email, Pivot additionally stores your email address and an encrypted password with Supabase. We use this to sign you in and to associate your usage with your account.

Automatic backup

Your goals and tasks are kept on your device, and Pivot also backs them up to our servers automatically, without you asking. The backup is on by default. It holds:

  • your profile
  • your goals
  • your milestones
  • your tasks
  • every task chat, with its messages

Temporary plan drafts are not included. We keep one snapshot per user, and each new upload replaces the previous one. The snapshot is stored on Supabase without additional encryption by us (plaintext at rest, protected by Supabase's own access controls).

You can turn the automatic backup off with the “Automatic backup” switch in the Profile tab. Your choice for this switch is stored with your account, so it stays the same on another device or after you reinstall the app. Turning it off stops automatic uploads and offers to delete the copy stored on our servers. If you keep that copy, it stays until you delete it or delete your account. Even while the switch is off, you can still create a copy yourself with “Back up now”. Guest accounts are not backed up.

You can delete the snapshot at any time in two ways: with “Delete server copy” in the Profile tab, or by deleting your account (see “Deleting your data” below).

Issue reports

You can send us an issue report from the app. It is a free-text channel, so please don't include anything you would not want us to read. A report contains:

  • the category you chose
  • the text you wrote
  • the name of the screen you were on
  • app version and build
  • operating system version
  • device model
  • app language and locale
  • your account identifier
  • your plan tier

A report is written once and is not shown back to you in the app.

Usage records and purchases

For each AI request we record how much of your monthly allowance it used: the token count, the model, and which feature made the request. We use this to enforce your plan's limit. If you buy a subscription, Apple handles the payment and tells us your subscription status. We do not receive your payment details.

This website

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

AI-assisted features

When you use an AI-assisted feature — such as generating goal ideas, a follow-up questionnaire, or chatting about a goal — the request is sent through our backend to Anthropic, the company that provides the AI model, so it can generate a response. What reaches Anthropic, field by field:

  • goal text
  • task titles and descriptions
  • task chat messages
  • your age
  • your motivators
  • your friction reducers
  • your daily time budget

We do not send an account identifier. In our default mode, all requests use one shared API key belonging to us.

Anthropic's Commercial Terms say that Anthropic may not train models on Customer Content from Services. By default, Anthropic deletes inputs and outputs within 30 days of receipt or generation, unless a longer period is required, for example for policy enforcement or by law. See Anthropic's retention page.

If you use your own Anthropic API key, the request goes directly from your device to Anthropic under your own account. Pivot does not receive it, and Anthropic's terms with you apply.

AI-generated content can be wrong. Check it before you rely on it.

Sensitive information you type

Goals, tasks and chat messages are free text. What you write may include sensitive information, for example about your health or religion. Pivot does not detect such information and does not build a profile from it. Under the GDPR, we do not rely on a special legal condition for processing it, so please share only what you are comfortable with. Where this text goes is described in this policy: it is stored in your backup and sent to Anthropic when you use an AI feature.

Why we use your data

  • Running your account, the automatic backup and the AI features: performance of our contract with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Account security, abuse handling and enforcing usage limits: our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Handling issue reports so we can fix problems: our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Who receives data, and transfers

We share data with these recipients, and no one else:

  • Supabase: authentication, database and server functions.
  • Anthropic: generating AI responses, as described above.
  • Apple: App Store purchases and subscription status.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Your data is stored in the EU (Stockholm region). Supabase and its sub-processors may process data in other countries, and Anthropic processes requests in the United States and other regions. Transfers outside the EU rely on the Standard Contractual Clauses. Supabase publishes its list of sub-processors, which includes OpenAI, LLC for natural language processing and generation.

How long we keep data

  • Account, account settings and backup snapshot: for as long as your account exists.
  • Usage records: for a rolling 12 months.
  • Subscription records: up to 5 years. When you delete your account, we remove the personal identifiers from them but keep the record itself.
  • Issue reports: until you delete your account.
  • Guest accounts that are inactive for 30 days are deleted.
  • Anthropic: up to 30 days by default, as described above.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and port your data, and to object to processing that we base on legitimate interest. To use any of these rights, email maksym.korostelov.dev@gmail.com. Erasure is also available in the app (see below).

You can complain to the Polish supervisory authority: the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, UODO), or to the authority in your own EU country.

Deleting your data

You can permanently delete your account and its associated server-side data at any time from the app's Profile tab — whether you're using a guest account or a registered one. Deletion doesn't require an email or password; the app identifies your account directly. See Delete Account for details.

Subscription records are redacted rather than deleted: we remove the personal identifiers and keep the rest of the record for up to 5 years.

Some copies are outside the in-app deletion: requests held at Anthropic for up to 30 days, server function logs kept by Supabase, and Apple's own purchase records.

Age

You must be at least 16 years old to use Pivot.
